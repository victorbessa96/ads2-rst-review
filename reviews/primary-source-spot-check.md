# Primary-source spot check

Checked directly on 2026-11-23 against newly fetched primary texts, independently of the literature subagent's summary.

## 1. Original RST sustained input

Russo, Susskind, and Thorlacius, **The Endpoint of Hawking Radiation**, [hep-th/9206070](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9206070).

Section 4, lines 173-179 of the retrieved text, prescribes

$$T_{++}(x^+)={\epsilon\over\lambda(x^+)^2}\theta(x^+-x^+_0),$$

with `epsilon` called “the constant energy flux,” and gives the exact logarithmic solution. Lines 195-197 distinguish the black-hole-forming regime and finite-duration flux. This is genuine prior sustained one-sided RST input, but its asymptotically flat energy coordinate and reflecting-boundary problem are not the Kim-Son past-asymptotically-AdS2 branch.

## 2. Earlier finite one-sided wormhole pulse

Hayward, Kim, and Lee, **Dilatonic wormholes: construction, operation, maintenance and collapse to black holes**, [gr-qc/0110080](https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/0110080).

Section VI, lines 215-243 of the retrieved text, uses a finite positive-energy step `F_+=Delta` for `0<=x^+<x_0` with `F_-=0`, and integrates the piecewise geometry. Lines 245-251 give the trapping-horizon branches and state that even an arbitrarily weak pulse eventually produces a spatial `r=0` singularity. This is prior finite-pulse wormhole physics in the classical CGHS-plus-ghost model, not the Kim-Son RST constant-`P` every-ray formula.

## 3. Kim-Son bibliographic scope

The [arXiv abstract for gr-qc/0404098v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0404098v2) confirms the title, authors, v1/v2 dates, and the explicit statement that the asymptotic geometry **in the past** is AdS2 before the wormhole evolves into a black hole.

These spot checks support the package's narrow novelty position: sustained and finite one-sided flux existed elsewhere in the literature, while the exact constant-Kruskal-`P` every-ray formula on the Kim-Son branch was not found in the thirteen-document bounded corpus. They do not establish exhaustive priority.
