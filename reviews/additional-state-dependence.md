# Additional state-dependence literature

**Yohan Potaux, Debajyoti Sarkar, and Sergey N. Solodukhin, “Hybrid quantum states in 2D dilaton gravity,” Phys. Rev. D 108, 125012 (2023).**

- arXiv: [2310.18745](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.18745), submitted 2023-10-28, v2 2023-11-30.
- DOI: [10.1103/PhysRevD.108.125012](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.108.125012).

The abstract confirms that the paper studies state-dependent semiclassical geometry in the two-dimensional RST model, including Hartle-Hawking, Unruh, Boulware, and hybrid states, with explicit attention to horizons, curvature singularities, and geodesic completeness. It is directly relevant to the broader question of state-dependent RST geometry. It is not the Kim-Son constant-Kruskal-`P` every-ray calculation and does not supply that formula or its finite-pulse cutoff.

This source was added after the 22:18 reviewer recommendation. It supplements, rather than expands, the thirteen-document bounded novelty search.

**Yohan Potaux, Debajyoti Sarkar, and Sergey N. Solodukhin, “Islands for black holes in a hybrid quantum state,” Phys. Rev. D 111, 026019 (2025).**

- arXiv: [2411.09574](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.09574), submitted 2024-11-14.
- DOI: [10.1103/PhysRevD.111.026019](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.111.026019), published 24 January 2025.

The abstract describes a regular causal-diamond geometry with an apparent horizon and radiation at infinity, and studies radiation entropy and islands. It does not establish the constant-Kruskal-`P` every-ray formula, the finite-pulse cutoff, or the cumulative-flux criterion. It reinforces the same scope rule: hybrid-state RST results cannot be promoted to arbitrary quantum states.
