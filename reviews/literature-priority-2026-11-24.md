## Findings

### 1. The zero-classical-flux geometry is implicit in Kim–Son

Kim–Son's state functions and general impulsive solution remain defined when the impulse amplitude is zero. The future quadrant then has the same hyperbolic critical curve used in this package. The new contribution is the explicit identification of that sector as the future Hartle–Hawking black-hole interior and the unified global analysis, not a new geometry.

Kim–Son–Yoon explicitly studies state adjustment after particle backreaction and also chooses a final black-hole state with `t_pm=0`. It is a direct predecessor for state switching and must be discussed, not merely cited.

### 2. The cumulative-horizon architecture is established RST/CGHS physics

Thorlacius defines the incoming mass moment and Kruskal momentum, obtains the apparent and event horizons, and shows that a finite infall leaves a shifted eternal black hole. The late solution depends on moments rather than the detailed incoming history. Our total-flux criterion is the Kim–Son-state extension of this structure, with the outgoing-state term, `Omega_s` edge, and measure-valued first-hit treatment. It is not a new general focusing principle.

The original RST paper studies a sustained profile `T_{++}=epsilon/[lambda(x^+)^2]`, different from the constant Kruskal-density source used here. The constant-`P` exact root and all-finite-ray first-hit formula remain plausible model-specific results.

### 3. Radiation requires full-stress and net-radiation separation

Kim and Son compute the full outgoing stress as

`-lambda^2 theta(x_0-x^-) + kappa/[4(x^--x_0+A/lambda^2)^2]`,

and explicitly subtract the incoming background to obtain the net radiation

`kappa/[4(x^--x_0+A/lambda^2)^2]`.

Our general-profile result now uses the same subtraction. The full asymptotic stress generalizes to every one-sided nonnegative flux with `E_tot < infinity`; the net outgoing radiation is `kappa/[4(y-E_tot)^2]`, and in the asymptotically flat frame it is the constant thermal value `kappa lambda^2/4`. The decaying term in the full sigma-frame stress belongs to the background subtraction, not to an independent negative-energy radiation flux.

### 4. Support-removal collapse and state dependence are not new mechanisms

Hayward–Kim–Lee already constructs wormhole collapse when ghost support is switched off. Potaux–Sarkar–Solodukhin establishes that quantum-state choices change RST geometry, horizons, throats, and completeness. Our contributions must therefore be presented as model-specific exact results, not as the discovery of support-removal collapse or state-dependent geometry.

### 5. Recent injection literature does not duplicate the unified result

Saito's multi-injection RST analysis concerns asymptotically flat black holes, island delays, and entropy. It does not reproduce the past-AdS2 Kim–Son branch, the two-sided future-set theorem, or the final mass-parameter identity. It does show that general injection histories are an active research area and reinforces the need to credit cumulative-momentum methods.

## Verdict

The work is **not entirely new**, and it should not be presented as such. No complete duplicate of the unified global theorem or final identities was found in the targeted check. The defensible claim is a set of exact, model-specific mathematical contributions built on established RST physics, with the two-sided global theorem, constant-flux first-hit formula, and final mass-parameter correction as the leading candidates for independent specialist review.
