# Recent literature spot check, 2025–2026

This note supplements, and does not enlarge, the original thirteen-document bounded novelty search.

## Yuuta Saito, *Page curves and island's delays in asymptotically flat 2d spacetimes with injections*

- arXiv: [2509.03997](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.03997), submitted 4 September 2025.
- DOI: [10.1093/ptep/ptaf174](https://doi.org/10.1093/ptep/ptaf174), published 4 December 2025.

Saito derives RST solutions for a general number of energy injections and studies apparent-horizon growth, island delays, and continuous Page curves. The setting is asymptotically flat RST with islands and entropy, not the past-AdS2 Kim-Son wormhole branch or its mixed-ray singular-wall criterion. It is the closest recent work for any future injection-to-island program.

## Arias and Fondevila, *Capacity of Entanglement and Replica Backreaction in RST Gravity*

- arXiv: [2603.09763](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09763).
- Journal: JHEP 09 (2026) 146, DOI [10.1007/JHEP09(2026)146](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP09(2026)146).

This is an eternal-RST-black-hole replica/capacity analysis, not a proof or counterexample for the present wall theorem. It is relevant only if generalized entropy or replica observables are attached to the wall.

## Espíndola, Jahnke, and Kim, *Islands and traversable wormholes*

- arXiv: [2510.21985](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21985).
- Journal: JHEP 08 (2026) 035, DOI [10.1007/JHEP08(2026)035](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP08(2026)035), published 5 August 2026.

This work uses nonlocal bath couplings and negative-energy shockwaves in AdS2 JT gravity to make wormholes traversable, computes QES backreaction, and studies boundary two-point functions. It is a different gravitational model and mechanism. It shows the type of boundary observable needed for a later RST wall-to-information project, not evidence for or against the RST wall theorem.

## Castro, Hollander, Martinez, and Verheijden, *The Effects of Near-AdS2 Backreaction on Matter Fields*

- arXiv: [2509.08046](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08046), submitted 9 September 2025.
- Journal: JHEP 05 (2026) 282, DOI [10.1007/JHEP05(2026)282](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP05(2026)282), published 26 May 2026.

The paper shows that massive-field two-point corrections require metric backreaction, while massless-field corrections are dilaton-controlled. This supports the package's refusal to transplant the RST wall result into JT or to claim a universal scalar two-point signature.

## Net assessment

No inspected work supplies the fixed Kim-Son cumulative-flux criterion, the state-pulse phase boundary, or the no-universal-energy-threshold theorem. These citations sharpen the next research gates rather than changing the bounded novelty position.
