# Primary source and redistribution policy

## Citation

**Won Tae Kim and Edwin J. Son, “Wormhole phase in the RST model,” JHEP 0408:006 (2004).**

- arXiv abstract: [gr-qc/0404098v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0404098v2)
- PDF: [arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/0404098v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/0404098v2)
- TeX source: [arxiv.org/src/gr-qc/0404098](https://arxiv.org/src/gr-qc/0404098)
- DOI: [10.1088/1126-6708/2004/08/006](https://doi.org/10.1088/1126-6708/2004/08/006)
- arXiv v1 submitted 2004-04-22; v2 revised 2004-09-08.

The paper supplies the RST action and constraints, the running `Omega` and its minimum, the past-asymptotically-AdS2 static wormhole, the one-sided impulsive source, and the boundary functions `t_pm`. The sustained-step and finite-pulse sources, every-ray first-hit laws, finite-pulse shutoff theorem, horizon corollaries, closing-window law, and observer constructions in this package are research extensions, not equations claimed to appear verbatim in Kim-Son.

## What is included here

The full TeX, figure, and source tarball are **not redistributed** in this public-facing package. `source-anchors.txt` preserves only the short equation fragments used by the lexical source checks, at their original line numbers. The full paper is obtained from the arXiv links above.

The last locally verified full TeX matched its tar member with SHA-256:

`8906ec46e685404da151c8b7d52a325c4053ee8c8f17964df7264c69baf58872`

That hash is retained as provenance, not as a claim that the external file was downloaded during this run. Verification in external-source mode checks these links and the retained anchor fragments. It does not claim a fresh tar-byte identity check.

## License

The arXiv abstract page links `view license` to the **assumed 1991–2003 license**, not a Creative Commons grant. That is the arXiv perpetual non-exclusive distribution arrangement and does not grant general remix/reposting rights. Removing the full source files avoids turning this repository into a broader redistribution of the paper. Any wider copying of the original TeX or figure requires author/publisher permission.
